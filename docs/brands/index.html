Why fit matters

A bigger audience isn't always a better one.

A large following tells you how many people might see something. Fit tells you whether they'll care. Reach still matters; it just isn't where we start.

  • Audiences that care

    Creators whose followers are genuinely interested in what you offer, not just large in number.

  • Content that feels natural

    Partnerships that sit comfortably in a creator's world, so audiences engage rather than scroll past.

  • Partnerships worth repeating

    The right fit makes long-term relationships possible, and they build more trust than one-off posts.
